    My Buddies Party Rules

    1. Reservations must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

    2. All parties require credit card info and a $50 deposit to reserve space. There is a 24-hour grace period to cancel the reservation and refund the deposit.

    3. No outside food or drink of any kind (except for cakes and cupcakes). If bringing a cake, please bring your own serving utensils to serve the cake.

    4. Please help us keep My Buddies clean. Cake fights, food fights, silly string, confetti, and pinatas are not allowed. Excessive mess may be subject to a $50 clean up fee.

    5. Regular time allotted for all parties is 2 hours. If you would like to reserve the room for longer, please contact us directly by phone and request additional time. Additional fees may apply.

    6. Smoking and vaping are not allowed within the building but you are welcome to use our outdoor smoking area.

    7. Parties are limited to a maximum of 2 hours.

    8. Approapriate attire is required at all times. We ask you respect this as we are a family and community oriented restaurant

    9. Please refrain from running and excessive horseplay out of courtesy to our other customers!

    If you would like to use our party room, please reserve the space 24 hours in advance. Our party room is used for overflow on busy days, and we cannot promise that the party room will be available if you do not reserve.


    Disclaimer: We do reserver the right to refurse service to any and all indivuals who are not following our resturant policies or for those who are causing excessive disturbances to the other resturant guests.
