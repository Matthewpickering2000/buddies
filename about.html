Our Company

Since September of 1991, Bob and Tracy poured their hearts into My Buddies Pizza, building it into a beloved cornerstone of our community. After 30 incredible years, they decided to step back, choosing to pass their cherished legacy to a new ownership group who, like them, fell in love with what My Buddies represents.

We're honored to carry forward their commitment to 'making the best pizza around using high-quality ingredients with no skimping.' Our promise to you, our loyal customers, is a seamless transition - maintaining the delicious flavors and friendly atmosphere you've always known and appreciated. We're truly eager to greet both our long-time patrons and welcome new faces, building lasting relationships not just between our team and the community, but with each of you who walks through our doors.

You can still count on us for delivery within approximately 10-12 miles of our shop. Just as Bob and Tracy did, we firmly believe in supporting our community through a variety of avenues, including numerous fundraisers and local contributions. My Buddies continues to be a family-run, Christian-based restaurant, dedicated to providing a fun, welcoming, and family-friendly environment. We truly look forward to continuing to serve you and hope every visit brings you joy.
